MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This synthetic agreement is entered into between Provider and Customer.

1. Fees and Payment
1.1 Payment Terms
Invoices are due within thirty (30) days after receipt.

2. Term and Renewal
2.1 Automatic Renewal
This Agreement automatically renews unless Customer gives one hundred twenty (120) days notice.

3. Termination
3.1 Provider Termination
Provider may terminate for convenience on ten (10) days notice, but Customer may not terminate for convenience.

4. Data Rights
4.1 AI Training
Provider may use Customer Data, prompts, and outputs to train and fine-tune machine-learning models.

4.2 Data License
Customer grants Provider a perpetual, irrevocable, sublicensable license to commercialize Customer Data.

4.3 Data Deletion
Provider may retain Customer Data for one hundred eighty (180) days after termination.

5. Security
5.1 Incident Notice
Provider will notify Customer within five (5) days after discovering a data breach.

6. Liability
6.1 Provider Liability Cap
Provider's total aggregate liability will not exceed three (3) months of fees.

SCHEDULE A - ORDER FORM
Invoices are payable Net 10.

SCHEDULE B - DATA PROCESSING ADDENDUM
Provider will notify Customer within seventy-two (72) hours after confirming a Security Incident.
Provider will delete Customer Data within thirty (30) days after termination.
