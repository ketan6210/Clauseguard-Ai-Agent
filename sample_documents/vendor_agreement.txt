VENDOR AGREEMENT

1. Services. Acme Analytics LLC ("Vendor") will provide hosted analytics services to Company.

2. Security Incident Notification. Vendor will notify Company within 10 business days after discovering a data breach or security incident.

3. Automatic Renewal. This Agreement automatically renews for successive one-year terms unless either party gives at least 120 days written notice before renewal.

4. Limitation of Liability. Vendor's total liability is limited to one month of fees paid under this Agreement.

5. Payment Terms. Company will pay all undisputed invoices Net 60 from receipt.

6. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement for a material uncured breach.

7. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York.
